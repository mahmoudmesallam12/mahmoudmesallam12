# Mahmoud Mesallam

**Entrepreneur · Founder & General Manager, Future Life**
📍 Al Ain, Abu Dhabi, UAE

I build and run a company in the water sector, and I use software to make
its operations faster and more measurable.

## What I do

- **Future Life L.L.C. (O.P.C.)** — Founder & General Manager. Manufacturing
  water purification, filtration and desalination products for the UAE market,
  with a team of around 60 people.
- **Masar (مسار)** — An in-house field operations and delivery tracking system
  built with React and NestJS, including route optimization from Excel data.

## Setting the pace

Future Life develops and owns its product lines, including
**Nabaa Al Muzn (نبع المزن)**, **Suqia Al Khair (سقيا الخير)** and
**Technology Seven (تكنولوجي سفن)**. These are original Future Life
products, available exclusively through us. We were the first to bring
them to market in this form, and we keep developing them, which is why
they have become a reference point for the segment.

## Background

Started as a technician in the water industry, then founded Future Life.
